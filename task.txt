Hi everyone, Recently,  several papers  have explored broad spectral families of optimizers related to HTMuon, where the update takes the form U\Sigma^pV^T. Some papers include:

Freon: explores the regime (p>1): https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11181
DynMuon: considers a dynamic (p) within the range ([-1,1]): https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.17109
A paper that studies how to derive an optimal value of p: https://hal.science/hal-05626222/document#page=4.87  inspired by Dmitriy’s paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04299
Soft Muon, which has recently been used for strong benchmark performance on X  https://github.com/nilin/contra-muon


These works are highly relevant to our planned HTMuon v2 project. In addition to exploring different choices of  p, many of them propose efficient alternatives to directly computing the SVD-based update.

Our  goal For HTMuon v2 is to derive insights from a theoretical framework similar to Dmitriy’s paper and develop an adaptive-p optimizer. Ideally, the choice of p could also be layer-wise adaptive. More broadly, we would like to understand, under the LMO framework, when different spectral optimizers should be preferred.
